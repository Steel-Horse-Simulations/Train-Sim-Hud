TSW Hud · real weather

Real-world weather sync

Fetches live weather from Open-Meteo for your train's real-world GPS position in-game, then smoothly blends the in-game weather to match over 59 seconds, repeating every minute.

⚠ In TSW's pause menu, set the Weather mode to Custom (not Dynamic) — otherwise the game's own weather system may keep fighting these updates.
