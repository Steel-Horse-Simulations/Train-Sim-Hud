Theme

Pick an accent colour for panels, headings and highlights across the app. The speedometer's over-speed colours (green/amber/red) and connection status colours always stay the same regardless of theme, since those carry real meaning.

Known Trains

Every loco the app has seen gets remembered here, along with a custom speedometer max for the Dashboard's speed gauge - useful since a fixed 100mph ring is nearly empty for something like a Class 08 and overflows instantly on anything genuinely fast. The suggested value (when shown) comes from the loco's own top speed the first time it was seen; type your own to override it.
